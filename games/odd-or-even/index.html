ODD or EVEN

No coin here — call it the street-cricket way. Then bat or bowl your way through a 5-wicket, 60-ball dash, one shot at a time, with commentary for every ball.

CALL THE TOSS

Call odd or even on the street-cricket toss. Get it right, and you choose whether to bat or bowl first.

PICK YOUR SHOT

When you're batting, choose from six shots — hook, cut, cover drive, straight drive, pull, or sweep.

PICK YOUR DELIVERY

When you're bowling, choose from six deliveries — yorker, bouncer, googly, inswinger, slower ball, or off cutter.

CHASE THE TARGET

Two innings, five wickets, sixty balls each. Post a total, then chase it down — or defend it — to win.

Every ball is decided the moment you play it — nothing is saved between rounds.
